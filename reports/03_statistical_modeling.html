7 Baseline limitations and next step

The standard binomial assumes review-level Bernoulli variation is adequately represented by the grouped counts. The numerical dispersion and influence diagnostics above show that this baseline is not adequate for final inference. The following Step 5C section implements only the pre-specified quasi-binomial, beta-binomial, and review-volume exclusion analyses. It does not run alternative release specifications, threshold sensitivity, or other Step 5D analyses.

8.2 Beta-binomial implementation and heterogeneity

The grouped beta-binomial fit uses glmmTMB::betabinomial(link="logit"). Its dispersion parameter is beta precision phi, with variance n*p*(1-p)*(phi+n)/(phi+1) and derived within-game correlation rho=1/(phi+1). Larger phi means less extra-binomial heterogeneity; the Binomial limit is approached as phi tends to infinity.

11 Step 5F: Wilson uncertainty and empirical-Bayes rating adjustment

Step 5F uses all 2,241 games with at least one review, including both free and paid games. The 759 zero-review games remain part of the 3,000-game market sample but have no observed reception outcome and are excluded from Wilson and empirical-Bayes estimation. No price, genre, platform, or release regression is introduced.

The 95% Wilson score interval quantifies sampling uncertainty without changing the observed point estimate. Its lower bound supports a conservative ranking but is not a posterior mean or an adjusted rating. The empirical-Bayes posterior mean instead shrinks low-denominator extremes toward a Beta prior estimated by deterministic maximum marginal likelihood. Priors estimated on games with at least 10 and 20 reviews are sensitivity checks applied to the same 2,241-game evaluation population.
