Step 6B.1 Attention Tier 模型冻结

四级 attention intensity 的简洁可视化审计

1 为什么放弃原始 count model

Step 6B 的原始评论数模型未冻结：review volume 极端集中，Top 1% 游戏占约 65% 评论量，删去头部后参数和价格曲线明显漂移。这个失败保留为方法记录。Step 6B.1 不再修复 count likelihood，而是把正评论游戏压缩为四个有序 attention tier。

2 Attention Tier 定义

Tier 1 为 1–9 条，Tier 2 为 10–99 条，Tier 3 为 100–999 条，Tier 4 为 1000+ 条。顺序固定为 Tier1 < Tier2 < Tier3 < Tier4。759 款零评论游戏仍由 Step 6A entry model 处理，不进入本模型。

8 Final Conclusion

Portfolio Summary
item summary
1 25.3% of sampled games received zero Steam reviews.
2 Among reviewed games, 49.2% were Tier 1 and 5.0% were Tier 4.
3 Review attention was extremely concentrated; the raw count model was not stable under the heavy tail.
4 Attention intensity was therefore summarized with four ordered tiers and an ordinal logistic model.
5 The proportional-odds assumption was approximate, while the pre-specified High-100 logistic check broadly agreed.

四级 ordinal model 回避了原始 count 模型被极少数爆款支配的问题，同时保留了 attention intensity 的顺序信息。代价是失去 tier 内的原始评论数差异。
