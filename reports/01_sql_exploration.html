Step 4A SQL Exploration

Steam 3,000-game market sample

1 Scope and semantics

This is SQL-first descriptive exploration. The market sample contains all 3,000 games, including zero- and low-review games. The model candidate sample is the 844 games with at least 20 Steam-purchase reviews. total_reviews is interpreted only as a player-attention/market-engagement proxy, never sales. positive_rate is observed Steam-review positivity and is used only when reviews exist. Prices are collection-time storefront snapshots rather than launch or lifetime prices.

Observed in this sample, 402 games (13.4%) are free and 759 (25.3%) have no reviews. Windows support is nearly universal; macOS and Linux support are much less common.

7 Full market versus model candidate sample

o;sample_name games free_share_pct median_current_price_usd windows_share_pct mac_share_pct linux_share_pct avg_total_reviews avg_log1p_total_reviews avg_positive_rate
Market sample 3000 13.40 4.99 99.97 14.63 12.10 359.82 2.1429 0.8180

The >=20-review restriction materially changes sample composition, especially free-game prevalence, observed price, genre mix, and attention. These selection differences must be retained as a limitation in later modeling.
