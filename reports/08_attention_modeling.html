The raw Step 6B count model remains a documented diagnostic failure and is not deleted. Step 6B.1 replaces it as the portfolio-level attention-intensity model by defining Tier 1 as 1–9 reviews, Tier 2 as 10–99, Tier 3 as 100–999, and Tier 4 as 1000+.

The cumulative ordinal logistic model uses the same 2,240-row positive-count price-complete sample, a newly estimated df=3 paid-price spline, ordinary log(days) covariate, free status, platform, and seven pre-specified genres. It converges with a full-rank design and valid probabilities.

Threshold-specific binary logits indicate that proportional odds holds approximately rather than exactly: several weak coefficients change sign, but there are no major direction reversals. The single pre-specified High-100 logistic robustness model broadly agrees with the ordinal model, including the paid-price probability shape.
